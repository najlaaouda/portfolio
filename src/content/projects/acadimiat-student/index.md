## Overview
Acadimiat (أكاديميات) is a training platform, and this is the app its students use. In one place they find and buy what they need, follow their courses, hand in assignments, sit exams, book sessions with consultants and collect their certificates. I designed the app as the UX/UI designer, in Arabic and right-to-left, for iOS.

## Five kinds of product
Acadimiat doesn't only sell courses. Each product is used differently once it's bought:

- **Training courses** run over weeks: lectures, assignments, exams and a certificate at the end
- **Live courses** happen on set dates, so the date matters as much as the content
- **Consultations** are appointments: the student books a slot, joins the session and keeps a record of it
- **Digital products** are files to download and keep
- **Bundles** combine the others, for example one course, two consultations and a file

## The challenge
All five had to be sold in one shop and paid for in one cart, then each one had to land in the right place after payment. A course also holds a lot: content, assignments, case studies, exams, notes, attachments and a community. All of it had to stay manageable on a phone screen, in Arabic.

## Information architecture
The app has four tabs: **Home**, **My library**, **Favourites** and **Account**.

- **Home** is for finding something new, with one rail per product type
- **My library** holds everything the student owns, split into courses, consultations and digital files
- Inside a course, tabs sit beside the content: **Assignments**, **Exams**, **Case studies**, **Notes**, **Attachments** and **Knowledge community**, each with a count

## Design decisions
**One library, split by what you bought.** Courses, consultations and files each get their own tab with a count, and one search covers products, trainers and consultants. A student never has to remember where a purchase went.

**Checkout that knows what comes next.** One cart takes any mix of products. If the order included a consultation, the thank-you screen leads with booking a slot. Tamara's four interest-free instalments sit next to card, PayPal and Apple Pay.

**Pick up where you left off.** Home opens on the course in progress and how far the student has got. Inside the course, "15 of 20 videos" stays above the content.

**One shape for every course.** Every course is built the same way: units, then lectures, then items (a video, an activity, a quiz or a PDF), each ticked off when done. What it takes to earn the certificate is stated up front.

**Exams without surprises.** Before starting, the student sees the number of questions, the time limit, the attempts left and their past scores, with a warning when only one attempt remains. During the exam, a timer and question progress stay in view.

**Corrections where the mistake is.** The review marks every answer right or wrong and shows the correct answer in place, including fill-in-the-blank and matching questions. Assignments come back the same way, accepted or rejected, with the trainer's notes and files.

**A consultation has a before and an after.** Students book from a calendar of free slots and join from the session's details. Afterwards, the recording, attachments and the consultant's notes stay in the same place, and booking again takes one tap.
