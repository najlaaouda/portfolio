## Overview
BNBstore is a B2B marketplace that connects buyers with verified suppliers around the world, starting with furniture and interiors. The platform doesn't process payments: buyers and sellers agree on terms, and a logistics partner handles delivery. I designed its marketing website: five page types, each at 1440, 1024 and 390 pixels wide.

## Two audiences, two journeys
The site speaks to two different people, so it splits into two journeys.

- **Buyers** need to trust the platform before they sign up. Their pages explain how sourcing works, step by step, and answer the obvious question about payment safety.
- **Sellers** need a reason to list, and to know what it costs. Their pages sell the reach, show the pricing, and walk them through creating their first listing.

The blog serves both, with articles on marketing and selling online.

## Design decisions
**One colour per audience.** Buyer pages use a warm coral and seller pages use violet. The layout, type and components stay the same, so it still reads as one brand, but you always know which side of the marketplace you are on.

**Show the product, not a picture of it.** Each step of "How it works" is shown with a piece of the real interface: the search bar over product cards, a supplier chat with a quote request, and the checkout with a choice of shipping company.

**Answer the trust question early.** "No payment processing" is the first of the three points in "Our Unique Approach", on a dark band of its own, next to trusted logistics partners and dedicated support.

**Pricing you can compare in one look.** Four plans side by side, from free to enterprise, with the enterprise column raised as a card, and every feature on one row across all plans.

**Articles that are easy to scan.** Every section ends with a short "Tip", and a sticky table of contents shows where you are in the article.

## Design system
- **Type:** DM Sans throughout, from 50px headings to 17px captions, with a 1.5 line height for body text
- **Colour:** coral primary `#FF7D7D` with two pink tints, a near-black `#202020`, greys from 50 to 900, and a dark navy `#111725` for feature bands
- **Spacing:** a 4px scale from 4 to 40
- **Shape:** pill buttons and corner radii from 4 to 12px, with one soft shadow for cards
- **Responsive:** each page is designed at desktop, tablet and mobile widths, with grids that drop from three columns to one and a menu that collapses on phones
